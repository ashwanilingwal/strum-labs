FSS Steel-String Acoustic Guitar
--------------------------------

Sound bank assembled by roberto@zenvoid.org for the FreePats project, using
samples from the "FS Seagull Steel String Acoustic Guitar" by FlameStudios.
  http://freepats.zenvoid.org/Guitar/steel-acoustic-guitar.html

Copyright 2008 Gary Campion
Modified 2016-2020 by roberto@zenvoid.org for the FreePats project.
Modified 2026 for StrumLab: reduced to the pitch centres the chord library can
reach, and re-encoded from WAV to FLAC (lossless, verified byte-identical on
round-trip).
