Electric Guitar FSBS (clean)
----------------------------

Version 2026-08-07

Sound bank made by direct sampling a Fender electric guitar (basic model) using an audio interface with dedicated instrument inputs.

Samples had been processed through an amplifier and effects rack to create a clean guitar sound. Direct (unprocessed) sounds and other variants are also available. Please visit FreePats web pages for updates and more content:
http://freepats.zenvoid.org/
